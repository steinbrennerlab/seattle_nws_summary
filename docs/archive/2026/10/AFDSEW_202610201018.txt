the area with low clouds and fog near the water in Whatcom,
Skagit and Snohomish counties. Clear skies elsewhere.
Temperatures at 3 AM/10Z were in the mid 30s to upper 40s.

Not much going on today weatherwise. Large upper level low well
south and light flow aloft over the area. Light northerly flow
in the lower levels not strong enough to prevent fog and low
clouds from forming especially near the Puget Sound. This
scenario is also favorable for the development of Lake
Washington stratus just after sunrise. All of the low clouds and
fog will be gone by late morning. Highs in the upper 50s to mid
60s. 

Little change in the upper pattern tonight. In the lower levels
flow going light offshore but not strong enough to prevent
patchy fog forming again in the early morning hours. Lows in the
lower 30s to lower 40s. 

Shallow low clouds and fog dissipating in the morning hours
Wednesday. The light offshore flow will add a couple of degrees
to the highs, mostly in the 60s. While not expecting any records
we are getting to the time of year where record highs can be in
the mid to upper 60s.  

Weak upper level trough developing offshore Wednesday night with
the trough axis near the coast by 00Z Friday. Nothing in the way
of precipitation with the approach of the trough, just an
increase in cloud cover. Lows in the mid 30s to mid 40s. Highs

.LONG TERM /THURSDAY NIGHT THROUGH MONDAY/...
Extended models in good agreement early on with the upper level
trough moving through Thursday night for a chance of showers
especially in the mountains. Zonal flow aloft Friday with
nothing embedded in the flow. 

Once again confidence is low in the weekend forecast with a wide
variety of solutions in the model ensembles. The solution with
the most members is a front moving through the area Saturday
night/Sunday morning. There is another group of solutions that
has the front brushing the area during the same time frame.
There is even a few solutions with an upper level ridge building
over Western Washington. There is good agreement that said upper
level ridge will build/be over the area Monday. At this point 
will go with the chance of rain Saturday, rain likely/chance of 
rain Saturday night into Sunday morning scenario with a drying 
trend Monday. Highs in the mid and upper 50s. Lows in the mid 
30s to lower 40s. If we get the ridging Sunday night there is a 
good chance Monday will be the coolest morning of the season. 
Felton

.AVIATION... 
Southerly flow aloft turning more southwesterly by late this 
afternoon/early this evening and into Wednesday. At the surface, 
many terminals reporting light and variable winds while SEA, PWT and 
PAE favoring north to northeasterly winds with speeds 4-8 kts. As 
winds pick up in speed slightly late this morning to 5-10 kts, the 
north to northeasterly direction will continue to be favored in 
general.

Widespread VFR conditions in place for much of W WA this early 
morning, however lower cigs already present at BLI and PAE bringing 
isolated IFR/LIFR conditions there. As of this writing, starting to 
see some fog development at OLM as well and would expect at least 
IFR conditions to emerge there by 14Z this morning. Universal VFR 
conditions expected to resume by late morning and remain that way 
for the rest of the TAF period. Model guidance is hinting at 
isolated low cigs/fog by early Wednesday morning, but confidence is 
low regarding which terminal or terminals may see it.

KSEA...VFR conditions expected through the period, although latest 
guidance suggests prospects of low cigs/fog near 12Z Wednesday. As 
stated above, confidence is low at this time and will wait to see if 
this solution persists in future hi-res runs. NE winds 4-8 kts this 
morning shifting more NW by afternoon. Speeds will remain within 
that 4-8 kt range for the period, although may lean toward the 
higher end of that range in the afternoon and early evening.
