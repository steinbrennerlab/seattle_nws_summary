.AVIATION...
Another round of steadier rain is rotating through the region this 
morning, bringing fairly widespread rain which is lowering ceilings 
and restricting visibility. Expect widespread MVFR conditions 
through the afternoon as this move through, with local pockets of 
IFR under heavier showers. As the day progresses, a winds become 
predominantly east or southeasterly. Showers begin to decrease as 
the low continues to lose strength over the area. With light winds 
and some clearing overnight, expect to see fog and low stratus 
development from around the Seattle metro area southward through KOLM 
and KCLS. Moderate confidence for the development of IFR conditions 
11-17z in these areas, with improvement into the late morning 
Saturday as the air mass begins to dry out at the low levels.

KSEA...MVFR conditions are expected in steadier rain this afternoon.

clearing trend, but this will result in IFR fog/low stratus Saturday 
morning at the terminal.

.MARINE...
Weak surface low pressure over the coastal waters will remain in 
place into Saturday before dissipating as high pressure builds 
across the interior of Western Washington early in the coming week. 
East or southeast winds across the coastal waters and near the west 
entrance to the strait will occasionally reach 15 to 20 knots over 
the next couple of days until the offshore low finally dissipates. 
Otherwise, winds into early next week look to remain rather light 
across area waters. 

Coastal seas generally 4 to 6 feet today and into the weekend. They 
are expected to build back to around 10 feet Monday as another 
larger swell train reaches the waters.
