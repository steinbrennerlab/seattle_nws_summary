.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH SUNDAY NIGHT/...
Patchy fog has dissipated early thus afternoon leaving mostly
sunny skies across Western washington. High pressure continues
over the area with afternoon temperatures in the upper 60s to
lower 70s. Areas along the water will likely see high
temperatures more in the low to mid 60s. 

A ridge will shift eastward tonight into Saturday, allowing a
trough and a front to move through the area. At this time, we're
continuing to see the bulk of the rain falling late tonight 
into the early morning, affecting areas along the coast and the 
mountains. Post front, we'll see a convergence zone set up along
the Snohomish/King county line for lingering showers. The high 
temperature will cool to temps in the upper 50s, to lower 60s. 

Cooler but dry on Sunday with lingering showers in the Cascades,
high temperatures will continue to remain in the upper 50s. 

&&

.LONG TERM /MONDAY THROUGH FRIDAY/...Latest ensemble guidance
shows an upper level low off the coast on Monday, barely
clipping our area, resulting in mostly a dry day, with areas
along the coast seeing a shower or two. This system will weaken
into Tuesday as it rotates away from our area, leaving western
WA in light and dry offshore flow. High temps stay in the upper
50s, with a weak front slated to enter the area Wednesday into
Thursday for a chance of rain.

KSEA...VFR cigs are to continue this afternoon and evening with
