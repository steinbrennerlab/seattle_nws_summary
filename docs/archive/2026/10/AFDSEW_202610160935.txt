.SYNOPSIS...A ridge will bring dry and warm conditions today. 
Wetter and cooler weather arrives on Saturday. Sunday remains

&&

.SHORT TERM /TODAY THROUGH SUNDAY/...High pressure and offshore
flow will lead to one more warm and dry day across western WA.
East winds have peaked overnight with 40-50 MPH gusts in the
Cascade ridges and 20-30 MPH in the gaps. Winds will continue to
ease today. Patchy, shallow, fog is forming once again this
morning, especially in areas where the wind is calm. The fog
will burn off late this morning. Temperatures will track warmer
than average with afternoon highs in the lower to mid 70s. 
Expect 60s near the water. 

Friday night/Saturday will be cooler and wetter as the ridge 
shifts east and a front and trough move in from the west. The
bulk of the rain will fall tonight into Saturday morning and
mainly affect the coast and mountains. Showers with a
convergence zone are expected Saturday afternoon near the
King/Snohomish line. A few sunbreaks are expected outside the
convergence zone with afternoon highs in the 50s to lower 60s. 

We're in between systems on Sunday for cool but dry weather.
Sunday morning lows will be in the 30s and 40s with temps near
freezing around the south sound. Highs will be mostly in the
50s. 33

&&

.LONG TERM /SUNDAY NIGHT THROUGH THURSDAY/...An upper level low
will spin offshore on Monday, with showers mainly clipping the
coast. This system will weaken as it shifts farther south on
Tuesday, with light/dry offshore flow across western WA. A weak
front dissipates as it reaches the region Wednesday night and
Thursday for a chance of light rain. 33
