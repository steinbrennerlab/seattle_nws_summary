.SYNOPSIS...
Upper level trough moving through the area today. Upper level
low moving south over British Columbia today and tonight will

Upper level low hanging out off the Washington coast through
Friday night will finally weaken and shift inland Saturday.

Upper level ridge building offshore Sunday moving inland Monday.
Atmospheric river on the backside of the ridge aimed at the

Highs in the lower to mid 50s warming into the mid and upper 50s

.MARINE...
Onshore flow returns to the area waters today. Northwesterly winds 
along the outer coastal waters will become breezy this morning and 
linger into Wednesday, with wind gusts approaching 25 kt at times. 
That said, inherited SCA looks good and will remain in place. 
Another frontal system will move into the waters tonight into 
Wednesday, increasing winds and seas across the area. Increasing 
onshore flow will bring a brief push of small craft westerly winds 
to the central and eastern Strait late this afternoon into this 
evening. New SCA will cover this. Southerly winds will also pick up 
along the Puget Sound waters tonight, with latest guidance 
highlighting a 60-80% chance of gusts exceeding small craft 
criteria. Even though the time frame for these gusts seems to be 
pretty limited, will opt to cover with an additional headline here 
as well.

Winds will ease again across the area waters on Wednesday as the 
accompanying surface low drops into the area and tracks southward 
along the coastal waters. This low will allow for flow to turn back 
offshore, with offshore flow persisting the second part of the week. 

Seas 4-6 ft this morning will increase to 6-8 ft by this afternoon 
and will persist at this height into Wednesday. Conditions slowly 
subsiding back to 4-6 ft on Thursday and remain that way into the 
weekend.
