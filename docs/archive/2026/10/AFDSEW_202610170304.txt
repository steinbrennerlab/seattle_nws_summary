.UPDATE...
Warm conditions have come to an end this evening as a frontal
system approaches the region, bringing a round of showers along
with it. Seas across the outer coastal waters will rise early
Saturday morning as the system moves over the region, resulting
in the issuance of a Small Craft Advisory for waves 10 feet or
higher across the outer coastal waters. Otherwise, the remainder
of the forecast remains on track with a return to more fall-like
weather over the weekend.

.SYNOPSIS...
Wetter and cooler weather arrives tonight into Saturday. Sunday
will remain cool with showers in the mountains. Conditions look

.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH SUNDAY NIGHT/...
A ridge will shift eastward tonight into Saturday, allowing a
trough and a front to move through the area. At this time, we're
continuing to see the bulk of the rain falling late tonight 
into the early morning, affecting areas along the coast and the 
mountains. Post front, we'll see a convergence zone set up along
the Snohomish/King county line for lingering showers. The high 
temperature will cool to temps in the upper 50s, to lower 60s. 

Cooler but dry on Sunday with lingering showers in the Cascades,
high temperatures will continue to remain in the upper 50s. 

&&

.LONG TERM /MONDAY THROUGH FRIDAY/...
Latest ensemble guidance shows an upper level low off the coast
on Monday, barely clipping our area, resulting in mostly a dry 
day, with areas along the coast seeing a shower or two. This 
system will weaken into Tuesday as it rotates away from our 
area, leaving western WA in light and dry offshore flow. High 
temps stay in the upper 50s, with a weak front slated to enter 
the area Wednesday into Thursday for a chance of rain.

.AVIATION...
VFR cigs and visbys have returned to western Washington this 
afternoon. Surface winds are light and generally south to 
southwesterly at 7 kts or less. Expect these conditions to continue 
into this evening. The next weather system will arrive overnight 
into Saturday morning, bringing light rain to much of the region. 
Cigs are expected to go down to MVFR between 07-09Z for all 
terminals except KOLM where IFR cigs are more likely. Rain will 
generally ease after 17Z with VFR cigs for the remainder of the day. 

KSEA...VFR cigs are to continue this afternoon and evening with 
southwesterly flow. The next weak frontal system will be arriving 
late tonight into tomorrow morning. The bulk of the rain will be 
done by 17Z, with isolated showers possible in the afternoon. Cigs 
tomorrow morning may become MVFR for brief periods of time, but the 
prevailing category will be VFR.

.MARINE...
A frontal system will move through late tonight into Saturday 
morning. Seas will be between 8-10 feet tomorrow, more so in the 
offshore waters. High pressure building over the waters Saturday 
afternoon will remain through Sunday. Another system will move by 
west of the coastal waters Monday. High pressure will rebuild over 
the area Tuesday with seas subsiding back down to 4 to 6 feet by 
Tuesday.

Inherited small craft advisory for westerlies in the Central and 
Eastern Strait of Juan de Fuca for Saturday still looks good and 
will remain in place. Given current forecast for seas, will also add 
a small craft advisory for the Outer Coastal waters as seas look to 
be steadily around 10 ft for that zone for much of the day.
