.AVIATION...
Northeast flow aloft continues over Western Washington today as the 
region remains between an upper trough east of the Cascades and an 
upper ridge centered well offshore. Already seeing some improvement 
in visibility at locations impacted by fog and low clouds this 
morning. Low clouds will lift and scatter by around 18Z. VFR is 
expected areawide this afternoon with valley fog and low clouds 
reforming again tonight. Temperatures will approach freezing Monday 
morning in the southern interior. While there is some uncertainty as 
to how low the temperatures will get, there is a possibility for 
isolated areas of freezing fog.

KSEA...VFR with clear skies today. Northerly surface winds will
