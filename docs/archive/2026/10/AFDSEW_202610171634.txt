Cascades. Precipitation will slowly dissipate in this area into

frontal shower activity. Skies clearing this afternoon with

highs near or slightly above normal, in the upper 50s to mid 
60s.

Interior. Lows in the mid 30s to mid 40s.

agreement with the upper level trough dropping down the 
California coast through Tuesday leaving Western Washington in a

Upper level trough approaching the coast Wednesday. Frontal

Front slowing down and splitting Wednesday night with what is
left of the system moving inland Thursday for a chance of

upper 50s. 

Possible post frontal convergence zone Thursday night. Weak 
upper level trough moving over the area hanging around through 
Friday. Chance of showers Thursday night becoming confined to 
the Cascades Friday. Little change in temperatures from 
Thursday's highs and lows. 

Quick note on the 77 degree day in Seattle Friday. This was the
second warmest day on record for this late in the year. The only
day warmer was the record setting 88 degree day back in 2022.
The warmest days on record from here until March are 74 degree 
days on October 17, 1989, November 3 2010 and November 4, 1949. 
77 degrees is the normal high in Seattle from August 19 through 
25. The normal high for Seattle yesterday is 60 degrees. Felton
