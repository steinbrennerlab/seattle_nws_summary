How often is the first week of October dry in Seattle? In the 
81 years of records at Seattle-Tacoma airport the first week of
October has been dry 14 years ( 17 percent ). It has occurred 
twice in the last six years, 2022 and 2020. Felton

.FIRE WEATHER...
Fire weather concerns remain minimal through the next few days.
Light offshore flow and warm temperatures this weekend could 
result in locations in the Cascades and adjacent valleys seeing 
minimum RH values approach 30 percent Sunday. This will be 
short lived with onshore flow returning Sunday night into 
Monday. This will boost minimum humidity values in addition to 
slightly cooler high temperatures. Felton
