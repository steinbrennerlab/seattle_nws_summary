.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH THURSDAY NIGHT/...
Dry with near-normal to slightly cooler than normal overnight
lows tonight. Lighter winds into Wednesday morning will allow
for more early morning patchy fog formation in sheltered
locations. Morning shallow stratus/fog should lift and scatter
late morning with another warm October day. A weak upper level 
system continues to be expected to brush the area Thursday. The
main impact will be more cloud cover and near normal
temperatures. High resolution ensembles like the REFS showing
very limited, shortlived showers mainly in the Olympics and
north Cascades Thursday afternoon and evening. 

&&

.LONG TERM /FRIDAY THROUGH TUESDAY/...
Ensembles in fair alignment with the overall pattern Friday 
becoming more zonal but dry Friday. For the weekend, ensemble 
clusters in good agreement with the overall pattern indicating 
an upper level trough moving across the NE Pacific and Gulf of 
Alaska Friday night, then shearing into a more consolidated 
trough moving into British Columbia Saturday and a trailing 
upper level low over the eastern Pacific. The grand ensemble 
showing good agreement on the overall idea of this upper level 
trough moving south and east through British Columbia itself or 
along west along the BC into Western Washington Saturday night 
into Sunday. Nearly half (48%) of the ensemble members favor 
the more direct approach with a more consolidated trough. The 
remaining 52% show either slower more northerly track or even 
offshore track for the trough. The more consolidated track would
result in a more organized, but likely weak system moving 
through the area Saturday night into Sunday, where the 
northerly/offshore scenarios would likely result in more limited
precipitation brushing the area Sunday or even delayed into 
Monday. The overall pattern early next week looks fairly evenly 
split with 49% of the grand ensemble showing some degree of 
ridging early in the week, and 51% some degree of troughing.
