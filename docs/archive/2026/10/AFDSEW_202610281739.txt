.AVIATION...
An upper low remains over the region with widespread 
rain showers across the area today and tonight. As a result, 
prevailing conditions are highly variable with IFR conditions under 
heavier showers and VFR conditions elsewhere. Southerly surface 
winds continue, with gusts to 20 kt through the morning. Confidence 
in wind speeds is a bit lower into the afternoon, but around a 50% 
chance of stronger gusts continuing into early evening. Otherwise, 
will be monitoring around a 15% chance for an isolated thunderstorm 
this afternoon and evening, but likelihood remains too low for TAF 
inclusion at this time. A convergence zone may maintain more robust 
shower activity and lower flight conditions near/south of KPAE but 
north of KBFI this evening. Otherwise, little change with shower 
activity through the period with increasing stratus potential 
overnight for most of the area.

KSEA...VFR conditions expected to prevail with gusty southerly winds 
through the evening. Showers remain around the terminal area and 
could bring a brief lowering if these move over the terminal. 
Otherwise, winds easing overnight with showers decreasing the the 
potential for MVFR ceilings increasing by 12z through the morning 
Thursday.
