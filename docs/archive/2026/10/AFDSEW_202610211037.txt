.SYNOPSIS...
Upper level trough offshore today and tonight will move inland
Thursday with not much moisture associated with the trough.
Temporary ridge over the area Friday. Lots of uncertainty in the
weekend forecast with a chance of a system moving through
Western Washington. Trailing upper level low could still be
around Monday before an upper level ridge builds Monday night
and Tuesday. 

&&

.SHORT TERM /TODAY THROUGH FRIDAY/...
Satellite imagery shows fog in the Snoqualmie and Snohomish
river valleys with some low clouds and fog near the water in
Whatcom, Skagit and Snohomish county. Mostly clear skies over
the remainder of the area. Temperatures at 3 AM/10Z were in the
mid 30s to mid 40s.

Low level offshore flow this morning will not be strong enough
to prevent a little more fog and low clouds from forming near
the Puget Sound in the next couple of hours. The layer will be
shallow with sunny skies across the area by mid morning. The
offshore flow will add a couple of degrees to the highs with
most places in the 60s. Not expecting any records but this time
of year highs in the upper 60s can be records. The forecast high
of 65 in Seattle would be the 10th warmest October 21st in 82 
years of records. 

Low level flow turning light tonight and remaining light into 
Thursday. Upper level trough offshore reaching the coast late 
Thursday increasing the cloud cover over the area Thursday 
afternoon but not producing any shower activity. Lows tonight in
the mid 30s to mid 40s. Cooler Thursday with highs in the mid 
50s to lower 60s. 

Upper level trough moving through Thursday night perhaps giving
the Cascades a shower or two. Mostly cloudy skies in the evening
thinning out a little overnight. Lows in the lower 30s to lower
40s.

Weak temporary upper level ridge moving through Friday for
another dry day. Light onshore flow in the lower levels with
morning low clouds becoming at least partly sunny in the
afternoon. Highs in the mid 50s to lower 60s. Felton

&&

.LONG TERM /FRIDAY NIGHT THROUGH TUESDAY/...
Upper level ridge weakening and moving east Friday night. 
Models continuing to have trouble coming to a consistent 
solution for the weekend. The ensembles favor a weak system 
moving through later Saturday into Sunday morning. Ensemble 
mean pops are slowly getting lower in the last three runs with 
now just chance pops for everywhere but the north coast. Upper 
level low associated with the front hanging around later Sunday 
into Sunday night keeping chance/slight chance pops in the 
forecast. There is better consensus with an upper level ridge 
building Monday or Monday night for a dry Tuesday. Highs in the 
50s through the period. If the ridge does build later Monday 
good chance we see some lows in the 20s Tuesday morning. Felton

As we head into the last third of October here are some current
October rainfall totals in the west. You don't see this very
often. Seattle 0.08 inches, Los Angeles 0.23 inches, San Diego 
1.04 inches, Palm Springs 1.17 inches, Las Vegas 1.19 inches, 
Phoenix 0.42 inches. Felton

.AVIATION... 
An upper level low continues to spin off CA with dry and stable 
conditions across western WA. The flow aloft remains southwesterly. 
Light and variable winds in place for all terminals this early 
morning except for HQM, where easterly winds ranging 5-10 kts are 
expected to remain in place into at least late morning/early 
afternoon. Widespread north to northwesterly winds expected by noon 
today although speeds expected to generally remain less than 5 or 6 
kts for the remainder of the TAF period. HQM may start the afternoon 
ranging 4-8 kts but winds there will also ease to around 5 kts by 
this evening.

Widespread VFR conditions in place over much of W WA this early 
morning, however starting to see hints of low stratus and fog for 
terminals along the eastern Sound very similar to Tuesday 
morning...so expect conditions to degrade there down into IFR/LIFR 
between 12-15Z and persist into the 18-20Z time frame. VFR 
conditions return for the remainder of the TAF period.

KSEA...VFR conditions as of 09Z, but a brief period of 
shallow/patchy fog is possible starting between 12-15Z for LIFR 
conditions before VFR conditions return after 18z. Light and 
variable winds this morning before becoming more NW this afternoon 
with speeds around 5 kt. Winds will shift northerly this evening 
while speeds remain the same.

.MARINE...
Weak high pressure will lead to generally light winds over the 
waters again today. Onshore flow will increase on Thursday as a cold 
front moves inland. Seas will build to 10-15 ft Thursday night then 
gradually subside under 10 ft on Friday. Since both winds and waves 
will meet SCA criteria for the coastal waters Thursday, went ahead 
and issued the headline with good level of confidence as models 
have been advertising this for some time. The flow will turn 
offshore this weekend as a thermal trough forms along the coast. Low 
pressure will weaken over the offshore waters early next week. 33/18
