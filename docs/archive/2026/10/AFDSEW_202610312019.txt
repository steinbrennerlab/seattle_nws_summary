.SYNOPSIS...
Drier conditions will settle into the region into Monday as an
upper low moves south and a ridge builds into the region. The

.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH MONDAY NIGHT/...
An upper level low will continue to weaken today as it moves 
onshore to the south, with split flow briefly developing aloft
over western Washington. This will allow conditions to dry out 
this evening as an upper level ridge builds in overhead. While 
cloudy skies will stick around, some sun breaks will help bring 
high temperatures back to seasonal normals generally in the low 
to mid 50s. Cool overnight lows in the 30s will bring areas of 
additional frost development to the area on Sunday morning while
moisture in the low levels promotes another round of morning 
fog. High pressure building into the Pacific Northwest will 
maintain dry conditions and seasonable temperatures through 
Monday, with periods of morning fog and afternoon sun breaks. As
the upper level ridge axis shifts eastward on Monday, ensembles
continue to show low chances of rain generally along the coast 
and over the Olympic Peninsula.

&&

.LONG TERM /TUESDAY THROUGH SATURDAY/...
Chances for light rain continue into Tuesday as a splitting
frontal system passes over the region. Wetter and more unsettled
conditions are expected to return on Wednesday and beyond as a
series of systems are set to move over the region. While
ensembles continue to show a fair amount of variability heading
into next weekend, the system Friday looks to be the strongest
and will likely bring breezy winds alongside widespread lowland
rain and mountain snow.
