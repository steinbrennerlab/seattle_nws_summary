.SYNOPSIS...
Weak ridging builds into Western Washington early Sunday. An 
upper trough digs southwards along the coast late Sunday into 
Monday. Ridging builds back into the northern Pacific Northwest 
Tuesday and Wednesday. Troughing returns late week. 

&&

.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH MONDAY NIGHT/...
The latest forecast remains on track this evening with no
updates. Drier conditions will continue to develop into tonight
as clouds taper off this evening. Mostly clear skies and 
lighter winds early Sunday AM will promote fog development, 
particularly in the usual areas and river valleys. 

A weather system approaches the west coast late Sunday and
Monday with the bulk of the energy digging southwards towards
California early next week. Dry conditions continue on Monday
due to the system being well offshore. Clouds will spread across
Western Washington during this period. Otherwise, highs will be
in the mid 50s to low 60s Sunday and Monday.

&&

.LONG TERM /TUESDAY THROUGH SATURDAY/...
Upper ridging develops over British Columbia Tuesday and 
Wednesday with an upper low positioned well south of Western 
Washington, mainly offshore of central California. Dry 
conditions are expected Tuesday and Wednesday. AM fog will 
develop again both Tuesday and Wednesday morning with relatively
light winds and clearer skies. In addition, areas of frost are 
expected each morning, mainly away from the water. Ensembles are
consistent in the return of weak troughing Thursday into late 
week, bringing precipitation chances to the area, although 
rainfall amounts are not heavy due to the weakening nature of 
any frontal system. Temperatures look to remain near normal 
during this period, with highs peaking midweek.

.AVIATION...
Low level onshore flow will gradually weaken this afternoon into 
tonight then become northerly or weakly offshore on Sunday. Light 
surface winds and low level moisture will lead to the formation of 
IFR/LIFR fog and low clouds on Sunday morning...especially across 
the Southwest Interior and south portions of Puget Sound. Low clouds 
will lift for another VFR afternoon on Sunday under varying amounts 
of high level cloud cover. Northerly surface winds Sunday afternoon 
5-10 kt. 

KSEA...VFR conditions tonight, continuing on Sunday. Confidence 
remains fairly high that some LIFR fog and low clouds will be around 
the terminal on Sunday morning before scattering out by afternoon. 
Elevated NE surface winds this evening between 10-14 kt should 
decrease tonight to 5-7 kt. Winds will shift N/NW Sunday afternoon 
and increase to 7-10 kt.

.MARINE...
Surface high pressure will build across the waters tonight into
Sunday in the wake of a frontal system that moved across the 
waters earlier today. A low pressure system passing southward 
well offshore will turn winds northerly or weakly offshore 
Sunday into Monday. Weak high pressure will remain across the 
waters into the middle of the coming week for rather quiet 
conditions. A larger swell train near 10 feet generated by 
active weather well offshore is expected to reach the coastal 
waters around Thursday.
