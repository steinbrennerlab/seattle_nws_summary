.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH SUNDAY NIGHT/...Showers continue to
taper off this evening, with a few lingering showers over
western Washington. An upper level low will start to move 
southwards and weaken, taking away the showery influence over 
western Washington. We'll start a dry and cool trend heading 
into the weekend, with afternoon temps staying in the low 50s. 
Overnight lows, especially tonight into Saturday morning, will 
cool, with areas in the Southwest Interior seeing below freezing
temperatures in the upper 20s and low 30s. Therefore, a Freeze 
Warning is in effect for those locations. A Frost Advisory has 
also been issued for the northern Olympic Peninsula and north of
Seattle as temperatures are expected to range between 32 to 36 
early Saturday. Take the proper precautions to protect
vegetation if needed. Besides the cold temperatures, should be 
dry for any trick or treaters. 

Dry and cool conditions through Sunday over the region. 
Overnight low temperatures will be flirting with freezing 
temperatures as well, in the low 30s. 

Mazurkiewicz

.LONG TERM /MONDAY THROUGH FRIDAY/...Ensemble guidance remains 
in reasonably good agreement for the first half of next week 
with an upper ridge remaining generally over the region for the 
start of next week. This will maintain mostly dry conditions, 
though a portion of ensemble members bring a weak disturbance 
into the ridge enough to bring some light precipitation chances 
(20-30%) west of Puget Sound. Another weak disturbance brings 
similar chances toward midweek. A more notable shift in the 
pattern becomes more likely late in the week with a stronger 
front approaching Western Washington, but it's worth noting that
there's significantly increasing spread in the potential 
evolution of the pattern by that time. Cullen

&&

.AVIATION...South to southwest flow aloft continues into 
Saturday as upper level low offshore digs southward into the 
Oregon and northern California offshore waters. The low level 
flow will turn light overnight. Coupled with areas of clearing, 
this will be conducive to the formation of IFR or LIFR low 
clouds and surface visibility locally below 3 statute miles by 
early morning. Low clouds are expected to lift and scatter out 
for most areas 18Z-20Z Saturday.

KSEA...Last remaining showers will pass through this evening before 
some clearing takes place near or after 05Z. With surface moisture 
and light winds, this remains a favorable scenario for IFR low 
clouds to form. Low clouds should scatter out to VFR again around 
20Z Saturday afternoon. Surface winds light and variable becoming 
E/NE under 7 knots toward morning.

.MARINE...Weak surface low pressure over the coastal waters 
will remain in place into Saturday before dissipating as high 
pressure builds across the interior of Western Washington early 
in the coming week. Southeasterly winds over the coastal waters
this afternoon will reach 15 to 20 kts at times, but should
remain under small craft advisory criteria. Winds look to remain
generally benign and light across the area waters through the
first half of next week. 

Coastal seas 4 to 6 feet through the weekend. Seas will build to
slowly build late Sunday into Monday, back around 10 to 11 feet
as a larger swell train approaches.
