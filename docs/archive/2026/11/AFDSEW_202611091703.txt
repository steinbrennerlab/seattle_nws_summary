.MARINE...
Winds will begin to increase across the coastal waters through the 
morning as the first of a series of frontal systems work their way 
across the coastal waters. Winds will peak over the coastal waters 
this afternoon and into the early evening over the interior Salish 
Sea with high confidence (70-near 100%) in wind gusts reaching gale 
force over the coastal waters and the East Entrance to the Strait of 
Juan de Fuca. The southeasterlies earlier in the evening will favor 
Small Craft conditions through Admiralty Inlet and into the Northern 
Inland Waters, then following the frontal passage late this evening, 
winds will turn southwesterly and ease briefly over the coastal 
waters, but remain elevated over Puget Sound and Hood Canal, which 
will have an advisory through early Tuesday morning.

The next system will be right on its tail with winds once again 
increasing over the coastal waters early Tuesday morning. Winds will 
persist through much of Tuesday and Wednesday as the associated 
front stalls offshore before weakening Wednesday night. This will 
allow for an extended period of gale force winds over the coastal 
waters and also over the northern Salish Sea waters through and 
north of Admiralty Inlet through Wednesday night.

Winds look to ease in the lull between systems on Thursday before 
the next strong low pressure system approaches the waters Thursday 
night. Models are showing a 60-80% chance of storm force gusts over 
the coastal waters late Thursday night into Friday with gale force 
winds likely over the remaining coastal waters. Winds look to peak 
during the day Friday but remain elevated through much of the 
weekend with additional systems moving through.

Seas are continuing to slowly ease this morning and should lower to 
around 10-12 ft today and tonight before increasing late tonight up 
to 17-19 ft on Tuesday. Seas remain there through much of Wednesday 
before beginning to ease Wednesday night into Thursday. Seas then 
look to rise significantly Friday into the weekend with high 
confidence (>60-70%) in waves exceeding 20 ft Friday night into 
Saturday. The ECWMF ensemble 50th percentile significant wave height 
forecast by Saturday morning is around 25 ft with the 75th 
percentile reaching near 30 ft.
