.SYNOPSIS...Drier conditions will settle into the region into 
Monday as an upper low moves south and a ridge builds into the 
region. The first half of next week will remain mostly dry, with
chances of precipitation increasing mid to late week as a 
series of systems approaches the Pacific Northwest.

&&

.SHORT TERM /TONIGHT THROUGH MONDAY NIGHT/...An upper level low
will continue to weaken as it moves onshore to the south, with 
split flow briefly developing aloft over western Washington. 
Cool overnight lows in the 30s will bring areas of additional 
frost development to the area on Sunday morning while moisture 
in the low levels promotes another round of morning fog. A Dense
Fog Advisory has been issued for portions of Puget Sound and the
Southwest Interior due to the expected fog development. Fog is
already beginning to develop in isolated river valleys, and is
expected to further expand tonight into Sunday morning. 

High pressure building into the Pacific Northwest will maintain
dry conditions and seasonable temperatures through Monday, with
periods of morning fog and afternoon sun breaks. As the upper 
level ridge axis shifts eastward on Monday, ensembles continue 
to show low chances of rain generally along the coast and over 
the Olympic Peninsula.

&&

.LONG TERM /TUESDAY THROUGH SATURDAY/...Chances for light rain 
continue into Tuesday as a splitting frontal system passes over 
the region. Wetter and more unsettled conditions are expected to
return on Wednesday and beyond as a series of systems are set 
to move over the region. While ensembles continue to show a fair
amount of variability heading into next weekend, the system 
Friday looks to be the strongest and will likely bring breezy 
winds alongside widespread lowland rain and mountain snow.

.MARINE...Shower activity was decreased this afternoon across 
the coastal waters and the threat for brief waterspouts has 
ended. 

A surface low pressure will continue to dig south today. High 
pressure will build over the waters tomorrow through Monday. A 
system will approach the coastal waters to the north, leading to an 
increase in winds over the outer coastal waters on Monday. Latest 
guidance suggests there is a 55-70% chance for wind gusts briefly 
exceeding 21 kt Monday morning over zones PZZ170 and PZZ173. High 
pressure will move inland on Tuesday. The next system arrives 
Wednesday. Winds will increase across the coastal waters; however, 
latest probabilities indicate a 40-50% chance for wind gusts meeting 
small craft thresholds. A stronger system will impact the waters 
Thursday into Friday, bringing stronger winds across the coastal 
waters and interior waters, with latest probabilities at 65-75% for 
winds above 21 kt. 

Seas 4-6 feet this afternoon, decreasing below 5 feet tonight. Seas 
will build on Monday 7-10 feet and will hover around this range 
through late in the week. With the stronger system arriving Friday, 
latest guidance suggests seas will build  up to 13-16 feet.
