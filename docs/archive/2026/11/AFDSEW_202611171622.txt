.MARINE...
Winds expected to increase across the waters today and will turn 
offshore this evening as a low pressure system offshore moves 
southwards across the NE Pacific. Additionally, offshore winds will 
be locally enhanced along terrain gaps, particularly through the 
Strait of Juan de Fuca and near Grays Harbor Tuesday night. Small 
Craft Advisories have been issued for the West and Central Strait of 
Juan de Fuca, with latest high-res guidance indicating a 70-100% 
chance of small craft winds. Winds across the outer coastal waters 
expected to reach gale thresholds this afternoon, for which a Gale 
Warning has been issued. Gusts may also briefly reach gale along the 
inner coastal waters Wednesday morning at times, especially the 
waters adjacent to the West Entrance of the Strait of Juan de Fuca 
and Grays Harbor County. As of this morning, the Small Craft 
Advisory for the inner coastal waters has been extended through 
Wednesday morning. Seas will briefly decrease to 10 feet or below 
this afternoon, before increasing to to 10-15 feet Tuesday night 
into Wednesday. 

As the low pressure system continues to push south into California 
and away from area waters, weak high pressure will build late 
Wednesday into Thursday for calmer marine conditions. An active 
weather pattern returns  this weekend into next week as multiple 
systems are expected to move over the waters.
