The forecasters

Each part of a forecast discussion is signed by the forecaster who wrote it. Most sign with an office ID number, a few with initials or a surname. A signature like 33/41 means 33 updated text that 41 wrote earlier.

The office does not publish which person uses which number, and this page does not guess. Everything here comes from the 163 archived discussions: which parts each person signed, when, and with whom. The notes on writing style are impressions from reading their sections.

Discussions have three desks. The forecast desk writes the short term (the next two or three days) and the long term (the rest of the week). The aviation and marine desks write for pilots and boaters. The 8 most active forecasters have their own colors; everyone else is gray.

Credits recognize notable forecasting in three ways: called it early (first to flag something that later happened), honest about uncertainty (flagged doubt that proved justified), and clear explanation (explained the reasoning especially well). An AI model judges each week once later discussions show how things turned out, at most two per kind per week, and each credit quotes the forecaster's own words. They are a running tally of good calls, not a ranking, and there are no demerits.
